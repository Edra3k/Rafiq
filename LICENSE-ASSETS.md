# Rafiq — name, character and artwork

The [MIT License](LICENSE) covers the **source code**. It does not cover Rafiq's brand and artwork:

- the name **“Rafiq”** as the name of this app;
- the **Rafiq character** — the ivory dome with its golden crescent, its look and expressions as a character;
- the **app icons** and **menu bar icon** (`NotchBuddy/Assets.xcassets/`, `NotchBuddy/PhoneAssets.xcassets/`, `windows/src-tauri/icons/`, `android/app/src/main/res/mipmap-*/`, `docs/media/icon.png`);
- the **sounds** (`NotchBuddy/Resources/sounds/`, `android/app/src/main/res/raw/`).

They are made from scratch for Rafiq: the character is drawn in code (`RafiqLook.swift`, `look.ts`), the icons by `scripts/make-icons.py` and the sounds by `scripts/make-sounds.py`.

## Where the code comes from

Rafiq is built on [Coucou](https://github.com/Louis-CFM/coucou) by Louis Raillé and on Coucou for Android by Rhytam Biswas, both under the MIT License. Coucou's own name, its Mochi character, its icon and its sounds belong to Louis Raillé and are **not** used in Rafiq.
